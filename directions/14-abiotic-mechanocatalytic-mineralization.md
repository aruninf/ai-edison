# Research Vector 14: Abiotic Mechanocatalytic Carbon-Mineralization Lattices

## Objective
Design structural composites that utilize ambient mechanical operating stress (traffic loads, vibration, aerodynamic flexing) to generate localized piezocatalytic and tribochemical surface potentials, driving spontaneous direct-air carbon dioxide capture and in situ mineral precipitation ($\text{CaCO}_3$ and $\text{SiO}_2$) along internal crack lines and stress paths, enabling inanimate civil and aerospace structures to permanently sequester carbon and increase in density and yield strength over decades of use with zero external energy.

### A. Piezocatalytic Surface Potential Generation
When mechanical stress $\sigma_{ij}$ deforms the embedded piezoelectric nanocrystal domains:
$$P_i = d_{ijk} \sigma_{jk}$$
The resulting electric field and open-circuit potential difference $V_{piezo}$ across the pore boundary is:
$$V_{piezo} = \frac{d_{eff} \cdot \sigma \cdot L_{crystal}}{\varepsilon_r \varepsilon_0}$$

For high-piezoelectric coefficient nanocrystals ($d_{eff} \ge 100\text{ pC/N}$) under typical civil structural loads ($\sigma = 5\text{ to }30\text{ MPa}$), local surface potentials exceed:
$$V_{piezo} \ge 1.5\text{ to }4.0\text{ V}$$
This potential exceeds the thermodynamic threshold required for water splitting ($1.23\text{ V}$) and direct catalytic activation of dissolved ambient carbon dioxide.

### B. Accelerated In Situ Carbonation Kinetics
Ambient air permeating through open-cell micro-capillaries supplies gaseous $\text{CO}_2$ ($420\text{ ppm}$). The piezocatalytic polarization accelerates the rate-limiting carbonic acid ionization:
$$\text{CO}_2(\text{aq}) + \text{OH}^- \xrightarrow{V_{piezo}} \text{HCO}_3^-$$
In the presence of interstitial calcium/magnesium ion reserves in the cementitious or ceramic matrix:
$$\text{Ca}^{2+} + \text{CO}_3^{2-} \to \text{CaCO}_3(\text{calcite}) \downarrow \quad (\Delta G^\circ = -48.4\text{ kJ/mol})$$

### C. Self-Limiting Stress Redistribution
As mineral crystals precipitate inside the micro-crack root, the crack gap closes ($w_{crack} \to 0$). The stress intensity factor drops, local strain relieves, and the reaction spontaneously ceases at that locus, preventing over-densification and directing mineralization toward newly stressed zones.
