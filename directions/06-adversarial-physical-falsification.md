# Research Vector 06: Adversarial Physical Falsification (The In Silico Red Team)

## Objective
Establish an automated adversarial digital autopsy protocol within the simulation pipeline to expose numerical artifacts, mesh singularities, boundary leakage, and physical failure modes before any candidate invention graduates to laboratory synthesis.

---

## 1. The Core Bottleneck: Simulator Exploits

High-throughput generative algorithms (Bayesian optimization, reinforcement learning, genetic algorithms, diffusion models) routinely find solutions that maximize simulation objective functions by **exploiting numerical discretization flaws** rather than discovering true physics:

1. **Mesh Singularities:** Infinite localized stress or field strengths caused by sharp geometric corners or inadequate mesh refinement.
2. **Artificial Energy Leaks:** Non-conservative numerical integration schemes that inject spurious kinetic energy or apparent over-unity thermodynamic efficiency.
3. **Unrealistic Boundary Conditions:** Zero-slip walls or infinite thermal sinks that cannot exist in physical fabrication.
4. **Tolerance Fragility:** Candidates that function in simulation under zero-tolerance conditions but fail catastrophically under $\pm 0.1\%$ dimensional variation or surface roughness.

## 3. Mathematical Auditing Protocols

### A. Second Law and Entropy Production Verification
Every proposed thermodynamic or energy-conversion cycle must explicitly compute non-negative total entropy production rate $\dot{\Sigma}$:
$$\dot{\Sigma} = \frac{d S_{sys}}{dt} - \sum_k \frac{\dot{Q}_k}{T_k} \ge 0$$
Any configuration showing $\dot{\Sigma} < 0$ at any phase angle is marked as a numerical discretization artifact and discarded.

### B. Defect Sensitivity Index ($S_d$)
Quantifies the drop in performance metric $\Phi$ when introducing physical manufacturing variance $\boldsymbol{\delta}$:
$$S_d = \max_{\|\boldsymbol{\delta}\| \le \epsilon} \frac{\Phi_0 - \Phi(\boldsymbol{\delta})}{\Phi_0}$$
Candidates with $S_d > 0.20$ for realistic micro-fabrication tolerances ($\epsilon = 2\,\mu\text{m}$) are rejected as lab-unviable.

## 4. Deliverable Outcomes

* **Automated Falsification Reports:** High-resolution logs identifying the exact physical or numerical reason 99.9% of generative concepts fail.
