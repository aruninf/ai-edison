# Framework: Universal First-Principles Evaluation Rubric

Every candidate invention concept must pass this questionnaire before resources are dedicated to intensive virtual sweeps or physical synthesis.

## Gate 1: Scientific Mechanism Clarity
1. **Underlying Law:** Which established equation or law of physics, chemistry, or information theory describes the core mechanism?
2. **Conservation Laws:** Can you draw a complete energy and mass flow diagram accounting for all inputs, outputs, dissipation, and entropy increases?
3. **No Unjustified Assumptions:** Does the proposal rely on unproven assumptions (e.g. room-temperature ambient superconductors, infinite thermal conductivity, zero-viscosity fluids at STP, perpetual motion)?

## Gate 2: Differentiation from Legacy Solutions
1. **The Legacy Baseline:** What existing technology, material, or system performs this function today?
2. **The Fundamental Limit:** What exact limit (Carnot bound, friction, electron leakage, chemical equilibrium, Landauer limit) does the legacy solution hit?
3. **Why Now / Why Different:** Is this truly a fundamentally different scientific mechanism, or merely an incremental optimization or software wrapper?

## Gate 3: Thermodynamic, Kinetic & Scaling Viability
1. **Dimensional and Physical Scaling:** How does the mechanism scale across dimensions, temperatures, and pressures?
   * If relying on surface phenomena, does the mechanism collapse at larger volumetric regimes?
2. **Theoretical Efficiency and Performance Bounds:** What is the theoretical maximum efficiency or capacity governed by physical laws?
3. **Operational Stability and Lifetime:** What degradation mechanism (fatigue, oxidation, thermal runaway, radiation damage) limits long-term deployment?

## Gate 4: Virtual Falsification Protocol (The 10,000-Trial Test)
1. **The Fatal Flaw Test:** What is the most likely physical reason this invention will fail under realistic conditions?
2. **In Silico Falsification Experiment:** What parameterized digital simulation can stress-test this hypothesis across 10,000+ variations in minutes or hours?
3. **Quantified Go / Kill Metric:** At what precise simulated value or threshold is the candidate declared dead and archived?
