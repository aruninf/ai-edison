Governance & Anti-Hedge Rules

The Hard Locks: RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md

Discovery intelligence is constrained by mathematical logic, conservation laws, and experimental falsification rather than consensus.

LOCK 01
Falsification First

Every surviving hypothesis defines the exact quantitative observation that kills it before the mechanism is presented. A broken mechanism cannot hide behind narrative.

LOCK 02
Magnitude Obligation

Every quantitative claim requires an exact number, a derived order-of-magnitude estimate, or an explicit stated reason numbers are unavailable. No silent vagueness.

LOCK 03
Banned Phrases

Words like "further research is needed", "promising", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", and "robust" are classified as structural defects and fail the build.

LOCK 04
10-Axis Diversity Matrix

Mechanisms must differ on at least 4 of 10 fundamental physical axes (substrate, length scale, energy scale, topology, symmetry, etc.) or they are merged as duplicates.

LOCK 05
Dead Hypothesis Protocol

If a hypothesis dies mid-derivation, it is never quietly rehabilitated. The carcass is preserved in the record alongside the mathematical killing blow.

LOCK 06
Zero Retrieval Lock

Zero web retrieval, papers, or patents during discovery. You are the instrument. Everything produced is derived in-session from first principles.
